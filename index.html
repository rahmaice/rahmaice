How We Do It

From Source to Your
Door, Perfectly Pure

Every drop passes through a rigorous multi-stage purification process to guarantee the highest water quality.

01
🔬

Reverse Osmosis

Water is forced under pressure through a semi-permeable membrane, retaining no salts, organic impurities, particles, or bacteria while allowing pure water molecules to pass through.

02
🦠

Micro-Filtration & UV

After RO treatment, water passes through micro-filters and UV treatment to eliminate all viruses, bacteria, and any remaining substances affecting colour, taste, or smell.

03
🧴

Bottle Hygiene

Our industrial bottle washing machines disinfect and clean every bottle internally before filling, ensuring complete hygiene and a safe, contaminant-free product.

Why Our Composition Matters

Rahma Ice water carries only the essential naturally occurring minerals your body needs — nothing more. With a TDS of just 28 mg/L, our water is exceptionally light and clean, meeting WHO guidelines for drinking water quality.
